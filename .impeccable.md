## Design Context

### Users
Internal teams and clients exploring a document knowledge base. They need readable answers, source verification, and manageable conversation history on desktop and mobile.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, approachable. The interface should make research feel focused and understandable.

### Aesthetic Direction
Adapt Team-S's left conversation rail, bounded transcript, multiline composer, and source access. Use Devin-inspired quiet neutral surfaces, restrained blue actions, generous breathing room, Chivo body text and Gabarito headings. Default to the system theme with a direct light/dark toggle. Keep document management and navigation visible.

### Design Principles
- Keep answers and sources central; reveal secondary controls when needed.
- Preserve the reader's scroll position during streaming.
- Make status, cancellation and failure visible without discarding partial answers.
- Keep core chat controls available on narrow screens and to keyboard users.
